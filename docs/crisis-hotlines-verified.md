## tr (Turkish) — Türkiye (Turkey)

=== INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION — Türkiye (Turkey) ===

**1. ALO 183 — Sosyal Destek Hattı — `183`** → **CONFIRMED**
Verified against the official government source alo183.aile.gov.tr (Ministry of Family & Social Services). Confirmed 24/7, free from any phone in Turkey, with the sign-language video line 0549 381 0183. This is a genuine national psychosocial/crisis support line (violence, abuse, child protection, social support). Source: https://alo183.aile.gov.tr/

**2. UNICEF Türkiye — Youth Mental Health & Suicide Prevention Helpline — `+90 312 586 80 00`** → **UNVERIFIED — DROP**
This is a misattributed number. Independent search shows +90 312 586 80 00 does NOT belong to UNICEF — it traces to Atılım Üniversitesi (a private Ankara university). UNICEF Türkiye's actual office number is +90 312 454 1000, and even that is a general office switchboard, not a documented suicide-prevention helpline. The Find A Helpline snippet repeats this number, but the underlying attribution is wrong. A wrong number on a crisis page is exactly the failure mode we must prevent. **Must be dropped.**

**3. Universal emergency number — `112`** → **CONFIRMED**
Verified across multiple sources (Defence Turkey, Wikipedia, multiple 2026 travel/emergency guides): Turkey has fully unified police/ambulance/fire/gendarmerie under 112, 24/7, free, English-speaking operators available. Valid as the always-available fallback. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/112_(emergency_telephone_number)

**4. Pembe Hayat Yaşam Hattı (trans-specific) — `+90 549 100 06 88` (0549 100 06 88)** → **CONFIRMED**
Verified via Kaos GL and Pembe Hayat's own site (pembehayat.org). Free mental-health support for trans & LGBTI+ people, weekdays 12:00–18:00, explicitly covers suicidal-thought crisis moments. Source: https://kaosgl.org/haber/pembe-hayat-yasam-hatti-telefonlarinizi-bekliyor and http://www.pembehayat.org/psikolojik-danismanlik-hatti

**5. SPoD LGBTİ+ Danışma Hattı — `0850 888 5428`** → **CONFIRMED**
Verified via SPoD's own official site (spod.org.tr) and Kaos GL. LGBTI+ counseling, psychological/legal/social support. (Note: hours vary by source — some list weekdays, the most recent SPoD source lists Fri–Sun 12:00–18:00. Number itself is solid.) Source: https://spod.org.tr/lgbti-helpline/

**Bonus check — ALO 182 exclusion:** CORRECTLY EXCLUDED. The official Ministry of Health source saglik.gov.tr confirms 182 is the Merkezi Hastane Randevu Sistemi (Central Hospital Appointment System), NOT a crisis line. Several Turkish blogs miscategorize 182 as a "Yaşam Hattı / suicide-prevention" line — they are wrong; the official ministry source overrides them. The researcher's exclusion was the right, safe call.

**Confirmation of the core accuracy note:** Türkiye has NO dedicated 24/7 national suicide-prevention short-code (no equivalent of France 3114 / US 988). This is accurate. 183 and 112 are the always-available national resources; the trans/LGBTI+ lines are business-hours community lines.
